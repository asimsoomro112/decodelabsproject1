The design process

Blueprint

Every pixel on this site was decided here first — discovery, wireframes, semantics, style, logic and audit. This page documents the kit's roadmap as it was actually followed.

1 · Discovery

How might we…

How might we help a busy learner choose the right course in under 30 seconds on a phone?

Persona — Sara, 21

Final-year CS student in Karachi. Studies between lectures on a mid-range Android phone with patchy mobile data. Skims instead of reading; decides from level match, time cost and proof of outcome. Leaves pages that make her wait or think too hard.

Empathy map

Says

"Just tell me which course is for me."

Thinks

"Is this too advanced for me? Will it load on my data?"

Does

Skims cards, saves interesting ones, compares durations, abandons slow pages.

Feels

Overwhelmed by choice; relieved when filtering is instant.

3 · Semantics

Landmarks give screen readers and AI a table of contents. Toggle Show landmarks above to outline this page's header, nav, main, article and footer with labels.

Landmark map

  • One h1 per page; headings never skip a level
  • header → nav aria-label="Primary" → main#main → footer
  • Course cards and benefits are article; filters live in an aside
  • Skip link, lang="en", descriptive alt / aria-label on every visual

4 · Style

Palette — measured WCAG ratios

Type specimen

Montserrat 700 — Headlines that hold the page

Montserrat 600 — Section titles and card headings

Open Sans 400 — Body copy. Warm, readable, and quiet enough to let the ideas speak. The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog 0123456789.

Open Sans 600 — Emphasis, labels and buttons.

Two families, three weights, fluid with clamp(). Fallback stacks carry size-adjust metrics so the layout never jumps while fonts load.

5 · Logic

Interactions (vanilla JS, one namespace: CourseHub)

  • Theme toggle — system / light / dark, persisted in localStorage
  • Pointer-following specular highlight on glass cards and nav (rAF, fine pointers only)
  • Breakpoint watcher — matchMedia at 768px/1024px highlights the matching device frame in the background
  • Courses — search, level chips, duration, sort; re-renders wrapped in startViewTransition; save toggles persisted
  • Course details in a native <dialog> with focus return
  • Join form — Constraint Validation API, inline errors, error summary with focus management
  • Home — count-up stats on IntersectionObserver; featured courses rendered from data.js
  • Glass toasts for save actions; nav pill condenses on scroll via sentinel observer
